01 — The place

A long low house between the marram grass and the cold bright water.

Halcyon was built as a weather station and kept most of its manners. The windows face the wind. The floors are the boards that were already here. Nothing about it apologises for being quiet.

Fourteen rooms sit along a single corridor that runs the length of the dune. Each one opens onto the same view and none of them share a wall with the road. Breakfast is at a long table and lasts as long as it lasts.

We do not have a spa, a gym or a conference suite. We have a kettle, a fire, and a path down to a beach that is usually empty.
